NARUTO TRUMP BATTLE — MATCH PRESETS & TEAM SELECTION SPEC
===========================================================

SOURCE DATABASE
- 120 unique character cards.
- Six tiers: Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, Level 4, Level 5, Special.
- 20 source cards in each tier.

MATCH PRESETS (CARDS PER PLAYER)
--------------------------------
QUICK MATCH — 10 cards
- Level 1: 2
- Level 2: 2
- Level 3: 2
- Level 4: 2
- Level 5: 1
- Special: 1

STANDARD MATCH — 15 cards
- Level 1: 3
- Level 2: 3
- Level 3: 3
- Level 4: 3
- Level 5: 2
- Special: 1

CANDIDATE POOL SIZE
-------------------
- Each tier independently gets either 15 or 16 cards, chosen randomly.
- Pool construction happens independently for each of the six tiers.
- The match draws twice the preset quantity from each tier.
- Drawn cards are shuffled and split evenly between Player 1 and Player 2.
- No card ID can appear on both teams.
- Each team receives exactly the preset distribution.

ATTACK STYLE STAT KEYS
----------------------
- Taijutsu: T_ATK
- Kunai: K_ATK
- Special: S_ATK
- Ninjutsu: N_ATK
- Genjutsu: G_ATK

SELECTION MODES
---------------
BALANCED META
- Uses all five attack styles.
- For each style, inspect up to the first five cards in descending ATK order.
- Select two distinct cards per style as Phase 1 anchors.
- Repeated cards are placed into the Hold list; continue down the top-five window
  until two distinct anchors are found or the window is exhausted.

DYNAMIC META
- Randomly selects three of the five attack styles per tier.
- For each selected style, inspect up to the first five cards in descending ATK order.
- Select two distinct cards per style; repeated candidates are held.

WEAKEST META
- Mirrors Dynamic Meta.
- Randomly selects three attack styles per tier.
- Uses ascending ATK order to select the weakest two distinct candidates per style.

RANDOM
- Randomly selects 15 or 16 cards from the 20-card tier.
- No stat-based ranking is applied to candidate-pool construction.

FIVE PHASES FOR META MODES
--------------------------
PHASE 1 — STYLE ANCHORS
- Search up to position five in each selected attack-style ranking.
- Add distinct anchors to Selected.
- Place duplicates and reserve candidates into Hold.

PHASE 2 — HOLDS & PROMOTIONS
- Promote eligible Hold candidates.
- Fill from the Container using the mode's direction (strongest for Balanced/
  Dynamic, weakest for Weakest Meta).
- Priority is Selected > Hold > Container.
- No duplicate card IDs.

PHASE 3 — ATTACK DENSITY
- For each tier, compare each card's five attack stats with the corresponding
  tier-wide style averages.
- High density: at least four attack stats at or above their style average.
- Medium density: two or three.
- Low density: zero or one.
- Balanced/Dynamic promote High, then Medium, then Low.
- Weakest Meta promotes Low, then Medium, then High.
- Continue until the pool has at least 13 cards or no candidates remain.

PHASE 4 — FINAL BONUS
- Fill remaining positions using a composite ranking based on:
  55% mean attack stat, 25% minimum attack-stat floor, 12% attack density,
  and 8% aggregate position across the five style rankings.
- Dynamic/Balanced favor stronger composite candidates.
- Weakest Meta mirrors the direction and favors weaker composite candidates.

PHASE 5 — VALIDATION
- Remove duplicates.
- Fill any missing slots from remaining eligible candidates.
- Ensure exact 15- or 16-card pool size.
- Shuffle the candidate pool before the final match draw.

FINAL TEAM ALLOCATION
---------------------
- For each tier, randomly draw 2 × the per-player preset count from its pool.
- Shuffle that draw and split it equally between the two teams.
- Verify no duplicated IDs and exact per-tier counts before proceeding to Captain
  and Vice-Captain selection.

SCOPE / PRESERVATION
--------------------
- This update changes match setup and roster generation only.
- It does not modify attack resolution, Captain/Vice-Captain bonuses, HP, shield,
  temporary HP, animation timing, or battle history.
- Selection audit is available during team selection.


CURRENT BATTLE SETTINGS (updated 2026-11-17)
- Starting and maximum team HP: 10.00.
- Shield accumulates from successful blocks and is never consumed automatically.
- During their own battle turn, a player may manually spend 2.00 shield to recover up to 1.125 HP (capped at 10.00) and temporary HP according to the existing recovery formula (maximum 0.50).
- Captain: multiply successful attack damage and successful block shield gain by 1.8×. An exact tie counts as a block and uses a 1.00 base shield gain before the multiplier.
- Vice Captain: multiply successful attack damage and successful block shield gain by 1.4×. An exact tie counts as a block and uses a 1.00 base shield gain before the multiplier.
- These role multipliers apply symmetrically to both players. Exact ties continue to count as successful blocks.
